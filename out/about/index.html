About TPANA

Senior expertise, working alongside your team.

TPANA is an independent technology consultancy based in Athens. We help leadership teams make difficult product and technology decisions and give delivery teams a clear way to act on them.

How we work

From the first decision to handover.

  1. Agree on the outcome

    Clarify what needs to change, who owns the decisions and which constraints will shape the work.

  2. Set a delivery path

    Work through the product, architecture and operating choices together. Agree on what to test or release first.

  3. Build with your team

    Review progress and risks as the work moves forward. Document decisions and give your team what it needs to own the service.
